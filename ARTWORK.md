# Lumina Stream visual refresh

Created with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as optimised JPEGs for the website. The original generated PNGs are retained in `source/assets/` and the generation library. The deployed pages use the JPEG files below.

## Final assets

- `source/assets/watching-tv.jpg`: couple watching television; animated homepage hero, editorial and order imagery.
- `source/assets/cinema-tv.jpg`: cinematic entertainment on a large television; platform imagery, movies and series, trial and editorial imagery.
- `source/assets/sports-tv.jpg`: football on a television; live sports imagery and editorial cards.
- `dist/assets/logo.svg`: code-native television/play icon. Header and footer wordmarks say Lumina Stream on one line.

The moving hero uses a slow CSS pan and zoom with a subtle screen-light effect. It is an animated photograph, not a video. The motion control and reduced-motion preference can stop the animation.

## Prompts

### Watching TV

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium ultrawide website hero photograph for an IPTV streaming brand. A couple viewed from behind relaxing on a contemporary dark sofa watching a very large television in a sophisticated living room at night, remote control visible in one hand, subtle warm lamp light and cool teal television light. Television clearly visible in the RIGHT HALF displaying a generic cinematic film scene with an actor silhouette on a rain-lit city street, no text, no brand logos, no recognizable movie or celebrity. LEFT HALF of the photograph is naturally dark quiet negative space for large white website headline overlay. Premium photographic realism, tasteful cinematic color grading, charcoal, deep blue, subtle warm amber accents, convincing realistic television and people anatomy. Wide horizontal composition 16:9 or wider. No mountains, no forests, no landscape wallpaper, no poster collage, no UI, no words or logos. The main subject must visibly be people watching TV at home.

### Cinema television

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one premium widescreen cinematic photographic visual for an IPTV and movie streaming website, 16:9. A sophisticated generic television screen viewed almost straight on in a dark contemporary media room. The screen shows an original cinematic montage composition: main central scene a mysterious actor silhouette on a rainy amber and teal city street, left edge subtle abstract stadium floodlights, right edge a close-up actor's expressive face in moody film lighting. This must look like cinema and television entertainment, not nature stock photography. Convincing large OLED television with thin black bezel and subtle reflections, remote on low console in foreground, tasteful premium lighting. No words, no text, no logos, no recognizable real actor or real film poster, no celebrity. Do not create an actual streaming website UI. High-end streaming campaign photography. Rich contrast, charcoal, teal and amber.
