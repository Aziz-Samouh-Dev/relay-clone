177|What is IPTV?
178|IPTV means Internet Protocol Television: television delivered through an internet connection instead of a satellite dish or traditional cable connection. You need a compatible device, a player and an active subscription from a provider with rights to distribute the content.
179|Which Lumina Stream plan should I choose?
180|Choose Duck, Edge, Raccoon or Trex for live TV, movies and series, or Ciné Pack for on-demand viewing. Select a term of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months and one, two or three simultaneous streams. All plan totals are shown in CAD.
181|What currency are the prices in?
182|All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD). Each card shows the total for the selected term and the equivalent monthly cost. Applicable taxes, final prices and any other charges must be disclosed before a live payment is accepted.
183|Can I use the TV I already own?
184|Possibly. Compatibility depends on your TV model, operating system, regional app store and the player supported by the service. Check the device guide before ordering. A compatible streaming device connected by HDMI may be an alternative.
185|Is 4K available on every channel?
186|No. Picture quality depends on the source, your plan, device, player, display and network. A 4K-capable television cannot turn every source into native 4K. Confirm the resolution of the channels you care about before buying.

440|Can I check a specific channel before choosing a plan?
441|Yes. Use the channel request tool to build a list of the channels, languages or programmes that matter to you. Ask support to confirm the exact lineup for your country and plan before you pay. Tell us your country and the exact feeds that matter to you.
442|Does an IPTV player include a subscription?
443|No. A player is software that plays a stream or playlist. Content access is separate, and some players charge their own fee. Only enter credentials issued by your authorised service provider.
444|What do I need before setup?
445|Have your device model, a stable internet connection, a compatible player and the connection details supplied by your provider ready. Install software from the official app store or developer source. Keep account credentials private.
446|Why does a stream buffer?
447|Buffering can come from a weak Wi-Fi signal, network congestion, device limitations or the content source. First compare another app on the same device, restart the player, try Ethernet where available and test a lower playback quality. If only one channel fails, tell support which channel and when it happened.
448|What should I include in a support request?
449|Share your device model, player name, a description of the issue, the affected channel or title, and when it happened. Never put your password, full playlist URL or payment information in a public message.
450|How can I check a provider’s content permissions?
451|Ask the provider which content it is authorised to distribute and in which countries. Confirm the business identity, service terms, refund policy and payment route before purchasing. A large channel count alone does not establish that a provider has permission to distribute the content.
452|Can I watch while travelling?
453|Country restrictions and account rules may limit access when you travel. Check the permitted territory and household rules before relying on the service outside your usual location. A VPN does not change the provider’s distribution rights.

495|All journal articles
496|IPTV delivers television over an internet connection. The player, content subscription and internet access are separate parts of the experience.
497|IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. Rather than arriving through a traditional cable connection or satellite dish, a stream reaches your device over your internet connection. That explains the delivery method; it does not tell you which channels a service includes or whether a provider has permission to distribute them.
498|A working setup usually has three parts: a device such as a television or streaming box, a player that can open the stream, and an active subscription or other authorised source of content. An internet subscription is a separate requirement.
499|An IPTV player organises and plays streams. Installing one does not automatically give you access to television channels, films or series. Some players have an additional purchase or subscription fee. Check that cost before comparing providers.
500|Your provider may supply a playlist address or a server address with login details. Those details can contain private account information. Enter them only in a compatible player that you trust and never post them in a public forum.
501|The source resolution, stream bitrate, your device, display and home network all affect what you see. A service advertising 4K does not mean every programme is available in native 4K. Ask about the particular channels and events you want to watch.
502|When comparing a service, test the same device and network you plan to use every day. A successful test on a phone does not prove that an older television or a distant Wi-Fi connection will work just as well.
503|Ask for the current channel lineup for your country, simultaneous stream limits, device compatibility, full price, renewal terms and refund conditions. Confirm the business identity and how support can be reached.
504|Ask the provider to confirm the content it is authorised to distribute in your location. A large catalogue, low price or polished website is not a substitute for that confirmation. Keep a copy of the terms and order information.

530|Test one thing at a time
531|Check the exact device model, operating system and available player before purchasing. Player availability and features can vary by country and model year.
532|Find the model number and operating system in the device settings or on its product label. A television brand alone is not enough to establish compatibility: different model years can have different software and app support.
533|If you use an HDMI streaming device, check that device separately from the television. The streaming device runs the player, while the television displays its output.
534|Look for a player in your device’s official app store and read its developer information. Confirm that it accepts the type of connection details your provider supplies. An app with a similar name is not necessarily the same product.
